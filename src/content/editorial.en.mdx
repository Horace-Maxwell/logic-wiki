## Writing and review

We check sources and arguments before editing Chinese and English separately. Original teaching scenarios are labeled separately from historical materials. AI agents wrote and reviewed this first edition; it has not received independent expert review. Each unit lists references, and the library records the scope actually consulted.

Maintenance proceeds through source checks, Chinese drafting and editing, English drafting and editing, semantic comparison, and page verification. Translations preserve facts and inferential strength without copying sentence structure mechanically.

## What Humanizer does

The project stores pinned copies of [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) 3.0.0 and [humanizer-zh-cn](https://github.com/holygeek00/humanizer-zh-cn) 2.9.1-zh.2, including their commit records and MIT licenses. The website sends no article text to these projects and calls no rewriting service at runtime.

Editing removes empty praise, promotional language, chat residue, and unnecessary repetition while keeping terminology stable. Useful teaching lists, contrasts, and formulas remain. Style rules must not turn “not all” into “none,” “supports” into “proves,” or introduce facts the author never established.

## Traceable changes

Review receipts bind the complete bilingual unit, sources, glossary, and upstream rules by hash. Relevant changes invalidate old receipts; the English record also binds its Chinese source version. Records identify the agent executor, review scope, and treatment of findings.

Automated scanning blocks placeholders and chat residue. Context-dependent wording needs a specific review rationale. No detection score establishes natural prose or an accurate claim. Agent self-review and independent expert review are different kinds of work.

## Limits of classification and evidence

This taxonomy is a learning tool. Entries may overlap or stand in subtype relationships; statistical biases, philosophical disputes, and discussion strategies are not all formal fallacies. Naming an argument still leaves its contextual diagnosis to explain. Exercises allow insufficient information, and identifying an error does not establish that its conclusion is false.

Case quotations retain their original language, with Chinese translations supplied here. Reconstructed arguments are our analysis, not words attributed to participants. Regulatory allegations, settlements, and independent scholarly conclusions are distinguished.

## Licensing and access scope

Original explanations do not reproduce long textbook passages or illustrations. Authors retain rights in their books, papers, websites, and upstream skills; individual records identify terms. Checking a table of contents does not count as reading a book, and reading an abstract does not count as evaluating the full paper.
